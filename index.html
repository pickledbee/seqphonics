Our Method

A method grounded in brain science

Developed from decades of research, our proven approach combines multisensory learning, sequential skills, cognitive reinforcement and confidence-building to change how the brain processes language.

Multisensory Learning

Engaging more than one sense at a time, so letters and sounds stick for good.

Sequential Phonics

Cumulative skills, from basic sounds to complex words, each step built on the last.

Cognitive Reinforcement

Strengthening the neurological processes behind fluent, automatic reading.

Emotional Transformation

Rebuilding confidence, so students stop dreading reading and start believing in themselves.

Research supported by the National Institute of Child Health and Human Development has shown that this kind of intensive, multisensory phonics instruction can help struggling readers' brains process words more like those of strong readers.
